The Georgia Magnetic Resonance Symposium 2025 was held at the Georgia Institute of Technology. Mark gave a talk on the applications of solution NMR spectroscopy to the lab's efforts of finding inhibitors of PRL3. He also shared preliminary insights from the SynZyme CURE, developed with Dr. Shainaz Landge.
